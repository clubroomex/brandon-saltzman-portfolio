## Problem Statement

In a competitive boutique fitness studio landscape, Loft Cycle Club needed to stand
out and optimize its balance sheet. We hypothesized that better visibility into the
right metrics could drive margin, client retention, and instructor performance —
but class scheduling, class mix, and instructor placement were all gut-feel
decisions with no data underneath them to test against.

## Rationale

Before we could fix anything, we needed to be able to test our hypotheses at all —
and we didn't have access to clean data capable of that. The priority was building
the measurement layer first, rather than guessing at fixes and hoping they worked.

## Approach

- Built API integrations with the studio's booking platform (Marianatek) and
  marketing platform (Brandbot) to get clean access to instructor and class
  performance data
- Designed 5 testable metrics usable across different scheduling scenarios
- Used those metrics to test and adjust underperforming classes — first by
  swapping in more experienced instructors, then by testing different time slots
- Proposed and built new instructor bonus tiers to shift incentives toward
  class-size performance and higher-margin formats (spin, reformer)
- Rebuilt front-desk operations: wrote new training guides, brought junior staff
  up to speed on why their role mattered for the studio's metrics, and handled
  instructor and client disputes directly

## Outcome

- As a whole, these changes contributed to a **30% increase in ARR in 2023**
- Gave the studio its first metrics-driven view of class and instructor
  performance, replacing scheduling decisions made on instinct
- Shifted instructor incentives toward higher-margin class formats
- Standardized front-desk operations and onboarding, reducing reliance on
  any one person's institutional knowledge
